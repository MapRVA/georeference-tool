# Administration

Users are designated as admins at the discretion of the host.
The Yesterdays admin panel is located at `/admin/`.
You will be prompted to login with your OpenStreetMap account, just like the rest of the site.
All content on Yesterdays can be edited in this panel.

## Etiquette

You are running an instance of Yesterdays for your area, cool!
This is an opportunity to build a community. As people discover the site and contribute metadata to it, they will feel a sense of investment and ownership.
After all, their work is what makes Yesterdays valuable.

With this in mind, I humbly make the following suggestions to you.

- **Do not edit user-contributed data.**
  Even if you disagree with it, or it has an obvious typo. If you do wish to edit e.g. a comment, I strongly recommend that you contact that user first to seek their permission.
- **Similarly, do not edit source metadata.**
  Please keep it faithful to the source, even if it is wrong. Georeference notes and comments will quickly get to the root of any discrepencies.
- **Find your power users, and enable them.**
  If someone starts contributing heavily to your site, get in touch with them and encourage them. Build trust quickly, and share administrative privileges once you can stomach it.
